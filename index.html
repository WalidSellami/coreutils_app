Privacy Guide & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how CoreUtils safeguards your digital privacy with zero data collection.

What tracking parameters are removed by the URL Disinfector?

CoreUtils strips over 140 surveillance tokens including Google Analytics (utm_source, utm_medium, gclid, gad_source), Facebook/Meta (fbclid, igshid), TikTok (_t, sender_device), Twitter/X (twclid), and e-commerce tracking from Amazon, AliExpress, and eBay, while safely preserving critical parameters like search queries, video IDs, and timestamps.

How does Direct WhatsApp work without saving a contact?

CoreUtils utilizes WhatsApp's official deep linking protocol (wa.me) with automatic international dial formatting and country code validation. Your conversation launches directly in the official WhatsApp app or web interface with zero phonebook pollution.

Is any link or phone number sent to external servers?

No. CoreUtils is 100% zero-backend and runs exclusively inside your browser. No telemetry, no analytical beacons, no cookies, and no database queries exist. Your data stays strictly on your device.

Can I install CoreUtils on my mobile or desktop device?

Yes. CoreUtils is configured as a lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA). You can select 'Add to Home Screen' or 'Install App' in your browser to launch it anytime with instant performance.

No links or numbers are ever sent to any server.
